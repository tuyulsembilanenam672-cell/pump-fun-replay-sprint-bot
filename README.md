A deterministic selective momentum grinder for the Pump.fun Replay Sprint: it trades 10 SOL of paper money on a 24-hour replay of pump.fun bonding-curve events. It buys only coins still rising near their own peak (price +2% to +50% over creation, within 5% of the peak since creation, at least 8 recorded buys, real reserve at least 3 SOL, age at most 3000 slots), 0.02 SOL per buy, at most 5 open positions, one round trip per coin. It exits on a +15% take-profit, a −10% stop-loss, a trailing stop (−6% under the peak once up +4%), or a 400-slot hold timeout. Every decision comes from the harness state and the slot number only — no clock, no environment, no unseeded randomness — so the two grading runs produce byte-identical action logs.
